## 📋 Production Guidelines

### **Technical Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) minimum
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Audio:** Clear, professional narration
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **File Size:** Under 100MB per video

### **Visual Style:**
- **Color Scheme:** Eco-friendly greens, blues, and earth tones
- **Typography:** Clean, modern fonts
- **Graphics:** Consistent with EcoCompost branding
- **Transitions:** Smooth, professional transitions

### **Audio Guidelines:**
- **Language:** Clear pronunciation in respective language
- **Pace:** Moderate speaking speed
- **Background Music:** Subtle, non-distracting
- **Audio Quality:** Professional recording equipment

### **Content Requirements:**
- **Accuracy:** All information must be current and accurate
- **Clarity:** Simple, easy-to-understand language
- **Engagement:** Interactive elements and visual appeal
- **Accessibility:** Subtitles in respective languages
